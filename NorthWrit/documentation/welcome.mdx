## What is NorthWrit?

NorthWrit is the AI platform for your organization. It connects to your team's applications, indexes their knowledge,
and makes it available to your team and their AI Agents.

NorthWrit gives you reliable answers from your own data and keeps that data private. Search your documents,
build custom AI Agents, research the web, run code, and more - all in one place.

## Editions

NorthWrit is available in these editions:

- **Community Edition (CE)**: Free and open source. Includes Chat, Internal Search, Web Search, Deep Research,
  Agents, Code Interpreter, Image Generation, Projects, and Plugins.
- **Business**: Adds Craft and other features for growing teams.
- **Enterprise**: Adds SSO, permission sync, groups and access controls, usage analytics, and more.
  See [Enterprise Edition](/admins/getting_started/enterprise_edition).
- **NorthWrit Cloud**: Hosted by NorthWrit. Includes Business and Enterprise features based on your plan.

## Why NorthWrit?

Why choose NorthWrit over ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, Google Gemini, or Glean?

- **Reliable Responses from Internal Knowledge:**
  NorthWrit indexes your knowledge with hybrid keyword and vector search and gives the LLM the most relevant context.
  Every answer cites its sources, so your team can check the facts.

- **Open Source:**
  NorthWrit is built for security, data privacy, and easy self-hosting. It is free to get started.
  Teams with a long-term GenAI strategy can extend or customize NorthWrit to meet their future needs.

- **Highly Configurable:**
  NorthWrit is flexible, so you can optimize the experience for your team. Plug in any LLM, connect only the data you need,
  and enable only the features you want your team to use.
